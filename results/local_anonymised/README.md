# Local clinical dataset — anonymised aggregate results

Source: retrospective chest CT and PET/CT examinations (2020–2025) of the Republican Specialised Scientific and
Practical Medical Centre of Oncology and Radiology, used under ethics committee protocol No. 58 of 26 May 2025
(research use only). **Images are not published.**

What is removed: images, names, identifiers, examination dates, DICOM UIDs, age and sex of individual patients,
scanner models, lesion coordinates and free-text notes. Examination dates are replaced by the order of the
examination within a patient (`E1`, `E2`, …). Patients are identified only by study codes (`LCxxx`).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `local_volumes.csv` | 30 CT volumes of 19 patients: modality, manufacturer, slice spacing, lung volume, automatic quality-control status and warnings, contrast phase, lung-mask agreement (lungmask vs TotalSegmentator, Dice), patient-level split |
| `local_reference_lesions.csv` | Lesion proposals and the final category after review by two radiologists (tumour, lymph node, uncertain – masked in evaluation, removed), volume, maximum diameter, SUVmax |
| `identity_linkage.csv` | Patient-code merge log: clinically confirmed links and two image-based merges with the recorded evidence (summary values of vertebral shape similarity, lung volume); per-pair similarity values were not stored |
| `platform_local_exams.csv`, `platform_local_lesions.csv` | All 30 local CT volumes analysed through the «UzLungMSKT» platform (analyse-from-dataset function, SenGuard-3D v1.0.1) and compared with the two-radiologist reference by `research/senguard3d/platform_local_eval.py`: per examination – reference tumours, detected, platform lesions by tier, lesions on tumour / node or uncertain / not confirmed, patient Lung-RADS, Dice, analysis time; per lesion – tier, match, size, density, Lung-RADS, classifier probability. Findings in non-annotated examinations are not assessed |
| `local_senguard3d_audit.csv` | SenGuard-3D audit (6 examinations, 7/7 reference lung lesions detected) and closed test (3 patients, 3/3); patient-level loss L |

Summary (from the dataset datasheet): 21 patient codes, 19 with chest CT; age 43–77 years (median 64); 14 men and
5 women; inter-reader agreement 22/23 lesions (Cohen κ = 0.94). The samples are below the minimum size required for
a statistical certificate (59 patients), so all local results are **descriptive only**.

Generated by `research/local_anonymise_tables.py`.
The platform detects lung lesions; whether they are malignant is assessed by the physician.
